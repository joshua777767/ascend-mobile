Build a premium mobile-first web app called Project Upgrade.

Project Upgrade is a strict AI whole-person transformation coach. It helps users upgrade their body, weight, muscle, skin, energy, sleep, discipline, workouts, and daily routine.

This app is for:

* Skinny people who want to gain weight and build muscle
* Overweight people who want to lose fat and feel better
* Fit people who want to maintain, get leaner, improve performance, and stay disciplined
* People who want better skin, higher energy, better sleep, less bloating, and more confidence

This should NOT feel like a calorie tracker.
This should NOT feel like a workout library.
This should NOT feel like a game.
This should feel like a real strict personal coach in the user’s pocket.

Core promise:
“Tell Project Upgrade who you want to become. It builds your daily schedule, workouts, meals, check-ins, coach reviews, and weekly adjustments to help you become that person.”

7. Meal Check-In
   User can type what they ate.

Coach replies with:

* estimated meal quality for their goals
* what was good
* what was bad
* what to fix next meal
* whether it helped or hurt today’s mission

Tone should be strict, short, useful, and safe.

Examples:
Fat loss: “This meal is not terrible, but the soda is wasting calories. Next meal needs lean protein, water, and no extra snacks.”
Skinny/bulk: “You said you want to gain weight, but this meal is too small. Add rice, eggs, milk, peanut butter, or a shake.”
Fit/maintain: “Solid meal. Keep protein high and don’t let random snacks push you off plan.”

11. Weekly Adjustment
    User enters weekly weight and progress.

For fat loss:

* if losing too slowly, suggest reducing calories slightly, increasing steps, improving protein, or fixing late-night snacks
* if losing too fast, warn about burnout and muscle loss

For skinny/bulk:

* if gaining too slowly, add calories, add a shake, increase meal frequency
* if gaining too fast, reduce calories slightly to avoid excessive fat gain

For fit/maintain:

* if consistent, keep plan
* if falling off, simplify schedule
* if workouts stall, adjust training

For energy/skin/sleep:

* review sleep, water, meals, caffeine, stress, and schedule
* suggest basic lifestyle habits, not medical cures

16. Safety
    Do not tell users to starve.
    Do not recommend extreme fasting.
    Do not promise guaranteed results.
    Do not give medical advice.
    Do not claim to cure acne, fatigue, bloating, or medical problems.
    Include disclaimer: Project Upgrade is not medical advice.
    For minors, eating disorder history, diabetes, pregnancy, medical conditions, serious skin issues, or serious health issues, recommend speaking with a qualified professional.

Fasting can only be suggested as an optional tool, like a 12–14 hour overnight fasting window, and only when appropriate.
