Example:

Tuesday: Legs
Wednesday: Rest

Fitness level rules:
- Beginner = simpler exercises, lower volume, form-focused
- Intermediate = normal volume
- Advanced = higher volume/intensity

PART 2 — Sport schedule

If the user plays a sport, let them enter their sport schedule during onboarding and edit it later in profile/settings.

Ask:
- sport
- practice days
- practice start time
- practice duration
- game days if any
- intensity: light / moderate / hard

Examples:

Boxing: Tuesday and Thursday, 6 PM, 1 hour, hard

Use sport schedule in:

- recovery advice

PART 3 — Calories burned from sport

Estimate calories burned from sport using:
- body weight
- sport type
- duration
- intensity

- “estimated”
- “roughly”
- “approximate”

Example:
“Football practice: roughly 450–700 calories burned depending on intensity.”

If intensity is unknown, use moderate.

PART 4 — Plan adjustment

If sport practice burns a lot of calories:

For weight gain:
- add a snack/shake reminder
- increase food guidance so user still hits surplus
- emphasize post-practice meal

For weight loss:
- count activity but do not encourage undereating
- still require protein, water, and recovery

For athletic performance:
- emphasize recovery, hydration, protein, sleep, mobility

Examples:
“You have hard football practice today. Add a post-practice shake or extra meal so you still hit your surplus.”

“You have back + biceps today and football practice at 4:30. Keep lifting earlier or reduce intensity so you are not cooked for practice.”
